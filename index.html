Widgets

On your Lock Screen, too.

  • A single medication, or today's whole list
  • Lock Screen widgets, too

Reminders and alarms

Rings, even on Silent.

Quiet once it's checked off.

Get a reminder at your time, and add an alarm on its own or as a backup. Alarms ring even in Silent mode or a Focus, unless you've already checked off the dose.

  • Tap Take Now on the alarm to check it off
  • Slide to stop snoozes it, for as long as you choose
  • Touch and hold a reminder to check off the dose from there

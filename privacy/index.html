Daily Dose

Privacy Policy

Effective November 5, 2026

The short version: Daily Dose does not collect, sell or share any of your information. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone. There are no accounts, no servers, no ads and no analytics.

What the app keeps, and where

To work, Daily Dose saves the following on your device only:

  • The medications you add, with their dosages, scheduled days, times and pill counts
  • Which doses you've checked off, by date
  • Any notes you write on calendar days
  • Your style settings, such as color, font and appearance

This information is kept in the app's private storage on your iPhone so the app and its Home Screen widgets can show it. It is never sent to the developer or anyone else.

What the app doesn't do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No data sent to any server
  • No analytics, crash reporting or usage tracking
  • No advertising and no third-party code that collects data
  • No selling or sharing of information with anyone

Notifications

Dose reminders and refill notifications are scheduled and delivered on your iPhone. They are not sent from a server. You can turn them off anytime in Settings > Notifications > Daily Dose.

Device backups

If you back up your iPhone with iCloud Backup or to a computer, iOS may include Daily Dose's data in that backup. Backups are handled by Apple under Apple's own privacy policy, and the developer cannot access them.

Deleting your information

You can delete a medication in the app by long-pressing it and choosing Delete. Deleting the Daily Dose app removes all of its data from your iPhone.

Children

Daily Dose is not directed to children under 13. Because the app collects no information, it collects none from children either.

Health information

Daily Dose is a reminder and tracking tool. It does not provide medical advice, and it is not a substitute for guidance from your doctor or pharmacist.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

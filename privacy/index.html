Privacy Policy

Nothing leaves your iPhone.

Effective November 10, 2026

The short version: Daily Dose does not collect, sell or share any of your information. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone. There are no accounts, no servers, no ads and no analytics, and the app contains no third-party code.

No accountNo serversNo analyticsNo ads

What the app keeps, and where

To work, Daily Dose saves the following on your iPhone only:

  • The medications you add: names, dosages, scheduled days, reminder and alarm times, snooze length, and pill counts with refill alerts
  • Which doses you've checked off, by date, and the time you took each one
  • Any notes you write on calendar days
  • Your style settings, such as color, font and appearance

This information is kept in the app's private storage on your iPhone, shared only with Daily Dose's own widgets so they can show it. It is never sent to the developer or anyone else.

What the app doesn't do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No data sent to any server; the app doesn't connect to the internet
  • No analytics, crash reporting or usage tracking
  • No advertising, and no third-party code
  • No access to Apple Health, your location, contacts or photos
  • No selling or sharing of information with anyone

Notifications and alarms

Dose reminders, refill notifications and alarms are scheduled on your iPhone through iOS. They are not sent from a server. Daily Dose asks for permission before using them, and you can turn them off anytime: notifications in Settings › Notifications › Daily Dose, alarms in Settings › Apps › Daily Dose.

Daily Dose may also ask iOS to wake it in the background now and then, only to keep upcoming reminders scheduled on your iPhone.

What others might see

Notifications, alarms and Lock Screen widgets can show a medication's name on your Lock Screen. If you'd rather they didn't, choose which widgets to add, and set Settings › Notifications › Show Previews to "When Unlocked".

PDF reports

When you export a report from the calendar, the PDF is created on your iPhone. It only goes where you choose to send or save it, such as Mail, Messages, Files or a printer. Once you share it, its handling is up to the app or person you shared it with.

Device backups

If you back up your iPhone with iCloud Backup or to a computer, iOS includes Daily Dose's data in that backup. Backups are handled by Apple under Apple's own privacy policy, and the developer cannot access them.

Deleting your information

You can delete a medication in the app by touching and holding it and choosing Delete. Deleting the Daily Dose app removes all of its data from your iPhone.

This website

This website uses no cookies, analytics, advertising or third-party scripts or fonts. Like any website, its hosting provider may process standard technical information, such as IP addresses, to deliver the pages.

Children

Daily Dose is not directed to children under 13. Because the app collects no information, it collects none from children either.

Health information

Daily Dose is a reminder and tracking tool. It does not provide medical advice, and it is not a substitute for guidance from your doctor or pharmacist.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Requirements

Daily Dose runs on iPhone with iOS 26 or later, including iPhone Duo.

FAQs

How do I add a widget to my Home Screen?

Touch and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen, tap the Paintbrush in the top corner, then Add Widget”, and search for Daily Dose. The Medication widget shows one med; the Today widget lists everything due today. To pick which med a Medication widget shows, touch and hold it and choose Edit Widget.

How do I add a widget to my Lock Screen?

Unlock your iPhone, then touch and hold the Lock Screen. Tap Customize, then Lock Screen, and tap the widget area below the time. Find Daily Dose and choose the round or rectangular widget, tap it to pick which med it shows, then tap Done. Tap the widget anytime to check off your dose. Lock Screen widgets take on your Lock Screen's colors.

Is there a guide in the app?

Yes. Tap the Widgets button on the Today screen, then "How do I add a widget?" for step-by-step instructions for both the Home Screen and the Lock Screen.

The widget isn't updating.

Open Daily Dose once, which refreshes the widgets. If it still looks out of date, remove the widget and add it again.

I'm not getting reminders.

Check Settings > Notifications > Daily Dose and make sure notifications are allowed. In the app, edit the med and confirm a "Time to take" is enabled and "Send a push notification" is checked. Reminders only arrive on that med's scheduled days, and only if it hasn't been checked off.

I forgot to log a dose yesterday.

Open the calendar, tap that day, and tap the med to mark it taken. The pill count updates the same way it does on the Today screen.

A past day won't open in the calendar.

Days before a med's start date aren't counted. Edit the med and set its Start date earlier.

My pill count is off.

Edit the med and type the correct number under Pills remaining. Update it whenever you refill.

How do I delete a medication or all my data?

Touch and hold a medication on the Today screen and choose Delete. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your iPhone.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone. Daily Dose has no accounts and collects no data. See the Privacy Policy.

Medical questions

Daily Dose is a reminder and tracking tool and can't answer questions about your medications. For those, talk to your doctor or pharmacist.
